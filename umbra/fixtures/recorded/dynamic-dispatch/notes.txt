Partial analysis. The graph side is fixtures/partial, captured in
internal/graph/testdata/partial-snapshot.ndjson, not the fixture app.

compute_total gains a parameter. Three things are true of the same run at once:

1. RateCard.apply and line_total are resolved exactly and must still classify
   exactly as they did before the evidence tiers existed: confirmed.
2. quote reaches the change through a method call the parser resolved by
   inferring the constructor's type, which upstream reports as type_inferred.
   invoice reaches it only through quote, so its own hop is exact and the chain
   it hangs off is not.
3. dispatch.py reaches compute_total four ways at runtime, through a dispatch
   table, through getattr and through importlib, and the graph draws not one
   edge for any of them. The four tests in test_dispatch.py that exercise those
   paths are absent from the field entirely. The report has to say the field is
   a lower bound rather than report a clean selection.

rate_table.min.json is one line over the provider's minified threshold and is
in a language the provider indexes inventory-only, so partial_failures is
non-empty and one language in the snapshot has no relations at all.

legacy_rates.cob is COBOL, which the provider tiers inventory-only. Measured
rather than assumed: an inventory-only file on its own does NOT produce a
partial failure. It produces a file record, a symbol and no relations. The
non-empty partial_failures comes from the minified JSON.

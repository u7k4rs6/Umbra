## 14.3 Light field, both schemes

In internal/report/assets/umbra.css and umbra.js:

- Replace the two-stop radial gradient with at least six stops on an ease-out curve so the glow reaches zero opacity by 85 percent of its radius. No visible rim in either scheme, with one source or with many. When several sources share the field, scale the radius, not only the opacity share.
- Day scheme: glow opacity at most half the night value; pools painted in an ink tone (derive from --ink at about 0.18 opacity), not grey. Banding must be gone; check with a screenshot at 200 percent zoom.
- Pools follow the sweep: when a node crosses into lit its pool fades out over the same 240 milliseconds; a penumbra tier shrinks it to the penumbra radius; scrubbing backwards restores it. The mask blobs must be keyed by node id and updated in applyState, not only drawn once.
- Screenshot night and day at seq 0, at the cut, and at the end, and commit the six images under docs/renders/ (move lightfield-01.png there too; nothing image-shaped stays at the repository root).
